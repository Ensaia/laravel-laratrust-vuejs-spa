Dashboard Typography Cheat SheetStick to one font family. Use a strict hierarchy to guide the user's eyes:
Dashboard Headers / Page Titles: 28 px - 32 px
KPI Numbers (Large Stats): 24 px - 28 px
Chart Titles & KPI Labels: 14 px - 16 px
Body Text / Standard Labels: 14 px - 16 px
Table Data & Chart Axes: 11 px - 12 px
